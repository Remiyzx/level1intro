💡 Cultural Note: Surname Comes FIRST in Chinese Names!

Unlike Western/English names (First name + Last name), Chinese names always place the Family Name (Surname) FIRST, followed by the Given Name.
E.g., In my name Yáng Zǐxián (杨紫贤): Yáng (杨) is the family surname, and Zǐxián (紫贤) is the given name![cite: 4]

💡 Fun Trivia: Did You Know?

1. Chinese has NO Conjugations or Tenses!

Unlike English or French (eat / ate / eaten / eating), Chinese verbs NEVER change form! You just add time words like "yesterday" or "tomorrow".
E.g., "Yesterday I eat rice" → Super logical & simple!

2. Malay & Mandarin Shared Words (Kata Pinjaman)!

Did you know Malay and Chinese have influenced each other for centuries?
• Teko → 茶壶 (Cháhú / Tea pot)
• Becak → 马车 / 三轮车
• Taugeh → 豆芽 (Bean sprouts)
• Lonceng → 钟 (Bell)

3. Cashless & High-Tech Lifestyle

In China, physical cash and credit cards are virtually non-existent! Everything—from ordering street snacks to riding the 350km/h High-Speed Rail (Gāotiě)—is done via smartphone QR codes.
